Cookie policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026 · Applies to: whichfoodtoavoid.com

The short version: we use one piece of browser storage to remember your cookie choice, plus whatever our hosting and security providers need to serve the site safely. Affiliate cookies are set by the partner (for example Amazon) on their own site, not by us.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to store. We also use localStorage, which works the same way but never transmits data to a server.

3. Your choice

The banner at the bottom of the page lets you accept all cookies or continue with essential cookies only. You can change your mind at any time by clearing site data in your browser — the banner will then ask again and you can accept or decline afresh.

Analytics cookies

We do not load third-party analytics on {d}. If we ever add privacy-friendly analytics, they will load only after you accept them — and this page will be updated first.

4. How to delete or block cookies

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies / Delete browsing data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Blocking everything will not stop our tools working — they run entirely in your browser.

5. Legal background

We follow the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR, which require consent before non-essential cookies are set and clear information about what is stored.
