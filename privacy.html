Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026 · Applies to: whichfoodtoavoid.com · Data controller: Kokal Operations Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, part of the ZAHA Lab network

In one paragraph: Whichfoodtoavoid is a static website. We do not run accounts, we do not ask you to hand over personal details, and we never sell data. Our tools run in your browser — anything you type into them stays on your device. We hold contact emails only for as long as we need to answer you, and we follow UK GDPR when we do.

1. Who we are

Whichfoodtoavoid is operated by Kokal Operations Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, part of the ZAHA Lab network. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website. You can reach us at hello@whichfoodtoavoid.com, and for anything to do with data protection at privacy@whichfoodtoavoid.com.

2. What we do not collect

  • Anything you type into our tools — generation and calculations happen in your browser and are never transmitted to us.
  • Device or browser fingerprints.
  • Keystroke, mouse or session recordings.
  • Payment details — we do not sell anything directly on this site.

3. What we do collect

3.1 Information you send us

If you email us or use a contact form, we receive your name, email address and whatever you write. We keep it to answer you and for our records, and delete it within 12 months of our last exchange unless we have to keep it longer for a legal reason.

3.2 Technical data

Like nearly every website, our hosting and CDN providers keep standard server logs — IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — for security, abuse prevention and capacity planning. Those logs are controller-operated by our providers (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare) and are not used by us to build profiles of individuals.

3.3 Cookies and local storage

We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage. Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them. Full detail is in our Cookie policy.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: be informed; access your data; have it corrected; have it erased; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing that already happened). Email privacy@whichfoodtoavoid.com and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or to your local supervisory authority in the EU.

9. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and we keep the amount of personal data we hold to a minimum — the most effective protection available. No online service can promise perfect security, but we will tell you if a breach affects your rights.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change anything material we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of whichfoodtoavoid.com after a change means you accept the updated policy.
