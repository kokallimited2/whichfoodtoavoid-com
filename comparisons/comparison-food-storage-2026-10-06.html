❓ FAQs

Can I put Vtopmart lids in the microwave?

No. The BPA-free plastic lids must be removed before microwaving. Only the glass containers themselves are microwave-safe, and you should place a microwave-safe cover loosely over the top to avoid splatter.

Is Tritan plastic safe for long-term food storage?

Yes. Tritan is a BPA-free copolyester certified food-safe by US and European food safety authorities. Third-party testing found no staining, pitting, or structural changes after repeated microwaving and dishwasher cycles.

Which set stacks better in a crowded fridge?

Rubbermaid Brilliance has a modular flat-lid design built for stacking. Vtopmart containers stack but the snap-lock clips add height, so tall columns become unstable in a packed fridge.
