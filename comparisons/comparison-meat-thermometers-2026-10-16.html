❓ FAQs

Is the ThermoPro TP03B waterproof?

No. The TP03B has no waterproof rating and should be cleaned with a damp cloth rather than rinsed under a tap. If waterproofing is a priority, the TP19H carries an IP65 rating and handles a full rinse under the kitchen sink without any damage.

How do the response times compare in practice?

The TP03B reads in 3-5 seconds; the TP19H reads in 3-4 seconds. Both are fast enough for checking steak or chicken at the grill, and the difference of a second or two is unlikely to affect your cooking.

Can these thermometers be used for candy making or deep-frying as well as meat?

Yes. Both models cover -50 to 300°C, which spans standard candy stages (hard crack sits at around 150°C) and most deep-frying temperatures. Neither is rated above 300°C, so very high-temperature sugar work that pushes past that point is outside their range.
