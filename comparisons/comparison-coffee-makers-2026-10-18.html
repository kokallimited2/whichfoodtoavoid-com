❓ FAQs

Are both machines SCA certified?

Yes. Both hold Specialty Coffee Association certification, confirming they brew at 197-205F, the range required for full extraction. Most sub-£100 machines do not meet this standard.

Does the Moccamaster have a programmable timer?

No. The KBG uses a simple on/off switch with no scheduling function. If you want coffee waiting when you wake up, the Bunn HB is the right choice.

Which is easier to repair long-term?

The Moccamaster. Technivorm sells individual spare parts and backs every machine with a 5-year warranty plus lifetime repair service. The Bunn covers 3 years only, with no equivalent repair programme.
