No — the Instant Pot Duo 7-in-1 has no air fryer function. If crisping and browning food is important to you, the Ninja Foodi OP350UK is the only option here with a dedicated air fryer crisping lid.

Which is better for a UK family of four?

Both handle four servings comfortably — the Instant Pot at 5.7L and the Ninja at 6L are virtually identical in practical capacity. The Instant Pot is the better all-round family buy on value; choose the Ninja if your family also snacks on air-fried chips and wings.
