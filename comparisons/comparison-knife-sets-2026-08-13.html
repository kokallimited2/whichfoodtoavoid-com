❓ FAQs

Are either of these knife sets dishwasher safe?

Neither set is recommended for the dishwasher. Both Ninja and D.Perlla advise hand washing — dishwasher heat and harsh detergents degrade blade edges and can damage handles over time.

Can I sharpen my existing knives using the Ninja block sharpener?

No. Ninja explicitly warns against using the StaySharp block sharpener on non-Ninja blades, as incompatible knives can become lodged in the mechanism and potentially damage both the knife and the sharpener.

Is the D.Perlla set worth buying if I already own a few good knives?

If you already have a chef's knife and a bread knife you are happy with, the D.Perlla's main appeal is the steak knife set and the sheer breadth of the collection at low cost. For someone who just needs one or two high-quality everyday knives, the Ninja's individual blade quality and guarantee may be a smarter long-term spend.
