❓ FAQs

What's the actual difference between the AF300UK and AF400UK?

The main differences are capacity and price: the AF300UK has 7.6L (2 x 3.8L drawers) at £149.99, while the AF400UK MAX has 9.5L (2 x 4.75L drawers) at £179.99. Both share the same DualZone technology, six cooking functions and 4.8/5 rating.

Can both air fryers cook two different foods at once?

Yes. Both use Ninja's DualZone technology with Sync mode (finishes two different foods at the same time) and Match mode (doubles up the same setting across both drawers), so you can cook, say, chips and chicken together.

Which one is better value for a family of four?

The AF300UK (7.6L) is the better-value choice for a family of four — its 7.6L capacity comfortably handles a full family meal, and you save £30 versus the MAX while getting identical features.
