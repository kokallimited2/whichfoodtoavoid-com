❓ FAQs

Can the Ninja AF400UK cook two different foods at the same time?

Yes. Its Dual Zone design has two separate 4.75L drawers, and the Sync function times them to finish together while Match copies one drawer's settings to the other.

Is the 5L Tower T17090 big enough for a family?

It comfortably serves a couple or a small family of three to four, but for larger households or cooking a main and side separately the Ninja's 9.5L dual-drawer capacity is far better suited.

Which air fryer is cheaper to run?

The Tower draws just 1400W versus the Ninja's 2470W, so it uses less energy per session, though the Ninja can cook more food at once, which can be more efficient for full family meals.
