❓ FAQs

Does the MEATER Plus work without a smartphone?

No, the MEATER Plus requires the free MEATER app (iOS 10.3+ or Android 5.0+) to display temperature readings. Without a paired phone or tablet in Bluetooth range, the probe cannot show any data.

Is the Alpha Grillers thermometer safe to wash?

Yes, its IP67 waterproof rating means it can be rinsed under running water after every use. Do not put it in a dishwasher, as the sustained heat and water pressure of a wash cycle can damage the internal electronics over time.

Can I use the MEATER Plus in a conventional UK oven?

Yes, it is designed for oven, grill, smoker, rotisserie, air fryer, and sous vide use. The only exception is deep-frying: the probe must not be submerged in hot oil, so it cannot be used for deep-fried turkey or similar applications.
