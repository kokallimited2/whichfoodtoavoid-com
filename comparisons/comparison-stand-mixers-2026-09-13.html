🏆 Verdict

For regular bakers who want a machine that will still be on their worktop in 20 years, the KitchenAid Artisan 5KSM125 is the stronger long-term investment — its 10-speed precision, planetary action, and enormous attachment ecosystem justify the premium. If you bake bread frequently, need sheer motor muscle, or simply cannot stretch to $367, the Kenwood kMix KMX750 is a formidable machine that outpowers the KitchenAid on raw watts alone and backs it up with a longer motor guarantee.

KitchenAid Artisan 5KSM125 best for enthusiast bakers who want design longevity, a wide colour range, and a versatile attachment ecosystem. Kenwood kMix KMX750 best for frequent bread bakers, budget-conscious buyers, and anyone prioritising maximum motor power over brand prestige.

❓ FAQs

Is the KitchenAid Artisan worth $107 more than the Kenwood kMix?

For bakers who use a wide range of attachments or value the KitchenAid's build quality and 10-speed precision over decades of use, yes. For pure bread-baking power or occasional home use, the Kenwood delivers better performance per pound spent.

Can both mixers handle heavy bread dough?

The Kenwood kMix handles stiff bread doughs confidently thanks to its 1000W motor. The KitchenAid Artisan manages most bread recipes well, but its 300W direct-drive motor may strain if you bake very dense or enriched doughs on a daily basis.

Are the bowls and attachments dishwasher-safe on both machines?

Yes — both the KitchenAid Artisan and Kenwood kMix include dishwasher-safe bowls, dough hooks, flat beaters, and wire whisks, so clean-up is straightforward whichever machine you choose.
