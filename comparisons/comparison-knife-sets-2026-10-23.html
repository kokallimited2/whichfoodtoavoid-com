❓ FAQs

Are both Astercook sets safe to put in the dishwasher?

Yes, both are labelled dishwasher safe. Hand washing in warm soapy water will preserve the blade edge longer, but occasional dishwasher use will not cause visible damage at this steel grade.

Does the 15-piece set's built-in sharpener actually sharpen the blades?

It hones rather than sharpens: it realigns the blade edge without removing metal. That extends intervals between proper sharpenings from around 4 weeks to 8-12 weeks of regular use. For full blade restoration you will still need a whetstone or a pull-through sharpener at some point.

Which set holds its edge longer?

The 15-piece, because 1.4116 German stainless steel is hardened to 55-58 HRC compared to the 6-piece's 3CR13 at 55-57 HRC. The difference is modest on paper, but 1.4116 is the benchmark steel for mid-range European knives and performs noticeably better under daily cooking over several months.
