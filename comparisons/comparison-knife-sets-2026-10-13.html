❓ FAQs

Are the Astercook knives safe to put in the dishwasher?

Astercook labels them dishwasher safe, but the anti-rust coating on the blades degrades faster with repeated dishwasher heat and detergent cycles. Hand washing with mild soap after each use preserves both the coating and the edge noticeably longer.

Does the AmazonBasics set come with a knife block?

No. It includes six individual blade guards rather than a block. The knives store flat in a kitchen drawer, which suits smaller kitchens but means you will need to buy a separate sharpener once the factory edge dulls.

Which set handles allergen separation better?

The AmazonBasics colour-coded set has a clear advantage for allergy-aware households. Each of the six knives has a distinct colour, making it straightforward to assign one blade per food type and reduce the risk of cross-contact between, for example, shellfish and vegetables.
