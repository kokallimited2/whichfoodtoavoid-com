🏆 Verdict

For most UK families the Russell Hobbs Inspire at £36 is the stronger everyday pick — it's more powerful, cheaper, offers independent slot control, and packs in a handy Lift & Look feature. However, if you plan to keep your toaster for a decade or expect heavy daily use, the Tefal Loft's near-double lifespan and 10-year repairability guarantee make the extra £4 a straightforward long-term investment.

Russell Hobbs Inspire best for budget buyers wanting feature-rich versatility, independent slot operation, and a wide colour choice to match their kitchen. Tefal Loft best for buyers prioritising longevity, consistent toasting results, and a premium-looking design they plan to keep for many years.

❓ FAQs

Can both toasters handle bagels, crumpets, and thick-cut bread?

Yes, both can manage crumpets and most thick-cut bread. The Russell Hobbs Inspire's self-adjusting slot width gives it more flexibility for irregular items. The Tefal Loft's fixed 3.5cm slots suit crumpets well, but very thick artisan slices may be a tight squeeze.

Does the Tefal Loft let you toast two pairs at different settings?

No. Despite having four slots split into two independent chambers, the Tefal Loft uses a single control dial for all four slots — you cannot run one pair at level 3 and another at level 6 simultaneously. The Russell Hobbs Inspire does support independent slot operation.
