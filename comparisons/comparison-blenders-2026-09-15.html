📝 User Reviews

BL610 owners consistently praise its ice-crushing power and XL pitcher for family use, with the main gripes being noise levels and occasional lid-seal issues over time. BC151 buyers love the grab-and-go convenience and leak-proof lid, though a handful note it can struggle with thick, oat-heavy smoothies.

🏆 Verdict

If you blend for more than one person or regularly tackle ice, frozen fruit, or soups, the BL610's 1000W power and 2.1L pitcher make it the better buy at just £15 more. However, if you're a solo blender who wants cordless freedom — at home, the gym, or the office — the BC151's portability, lighter weight, and superior 2-year warranty make it the smarter, more practical choice.

Ninja Professional BL610 best for families, fitness enthusiasts, and home cooks who need powerful large-batch blending at a permanent kitchen spot. Ninja Blast BC151NV best for solo commuters, gym-goers, and office workers who want grab-and-go smoothies without needing a plug socket.
