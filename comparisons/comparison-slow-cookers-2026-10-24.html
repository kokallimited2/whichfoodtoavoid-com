❓ FAQs

Are both pressure cookers compatible with UK mains voltage?

Yes. Both the Instant Pot Duo and COSORI 9-in-1 sold in the UK operate at 220-240V/50Hz and require no voltage adaptor. If ordering from a non-UK seller, confirm the listing specifies a UK Type G plug.

Which works better as a slow cooker?

Both include a slow cook mode, but neither exactly replicates a dedicated slow cooker. The Instant Pot's slow cook function runs slightly hotter than a Crock-Pot; set it to 'Less' and add around 15% extra liquid. The COSORI behaves similarly. If slow cooking is your main use, a dedicated slow cooker under £30 will outperform both.

Does the COSORI's lower maximum pressure matter in practice?

Rarely. The COSORI tops out at 10 PSI versus the Instant Pot's 11.6 PSI. For everyday recipes including beans, rice, soups, and chicken, the difference amounts to under 5 extra minutes. Only very dense cuts of meat cooked at full pressure for long periods will show a measurable gap.
