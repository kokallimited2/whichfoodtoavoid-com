❓ FAQs

Yes — both can. The Ninja has a dedicated Sear/Sauté function in its pot, while the Crockpot's die-cast pot is hob-safe (including induction) so you can brown directly on the stove before slow cooking.

Which one is better for a large family?

The Ninja Foodi PossibleCooker, with its 8L capacity, is far better suited to large families and batch cooking. The Crockpot's 5L pot serves up to about 5 people, making it better for smaller households.

Are the non-stick coatings safe?

Yes. The Crockpot uses a DuraCeramic coating that is PFOA and PTFE free, while the Ninja's pot is oven-safe up to 240°C and designed for durable everyday use. Both are dishwasher-friendly for easy cleaning.
