📝 User Reviews

Reviewers praise both scales as accurate, easy to read and excellent value, frequently highlighting Salter's long 15-year guarantee. Occasional complaints mention fiddly buttons and the faff of the coin-cell battery, but both average a strong 4.6/5.

🏆 Verdict

Buy the Salter 1036 if you bake in larger quantities or often weigh heavy bowls — the extra 2kg of capacity and rugged stainless steel platform are worth the small price bump. Choose the Arc if counter and drawer space is tight or you want the cheapest reliable option, as its slim design and 3kg limit cover everyday cooking comfortably.

Salter 1036 best for keen bakers and batch cooks who need 5kg capacity and a robust platform. Salter Arc best for smaller kitchens and casual cooks who prioritise slim storage and a lower price.

Yes, both scales ship with the required battery, so you can weigh straight out of the box.
