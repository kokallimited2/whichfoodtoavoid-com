❓ FAQs

Is the Vitamix Ascent X4 available with its full warranty in the UK?

Yes. It is sold directly through Vitamix UK and through authorised retailers such as John Lewis and Lakeland at £699.95. The 10-year warranty is valid for UK buyers, covering parts and return shipping.

Can the Ninja Mega Kitchen System make hot soup?

No. The pitcher is not designed for hot liquids. Blending hot soup creates a pressure build-up that can force the lid off. Blend cold or room-temperature ingredients, then heat the soup in a pan or microwave separately.

Which blender is quieter?

Neither is quiet. The Vitamix Ascent X4 measures around 101 dB at full speed; the Ninja BL770 scores 'Fair' on noise in Consumer Reports testing and exceeds 100 dB when crushing ice. Both are best used away from open-plan bedrooms or shared walls.
