❓ FAQs

Can both work as a proper slow cooker?

Both have a slow cook mode, but neither fully replicates a traditional Crock-Pot because they heat from the bottom rather than wrapping warmth around the pot. Use the 'Less' heat setting and add a splash more liquid than your recipe calls for — results are very close to a dedicated slow cooker for stews, casseroles, and pulled pork.

Is the Duo Plus worth $35 more than the Duo 7-in-1?

For most cooks, no. The pressure-cooking and slow-cooking performance is identical between the two models. The Duo Plus justifies its premium if you regularly sterilise baby equipment or glass jars, or if you will genuinely use the cake and egg presets rather than treat them as occasional novelties.

What can the 9-in-1 Duo Plus do that the 7-in-1 cannot?

The Duo Plus adds three extra programmes: a steriliser (ideal for baby bottles, utensils, and jam jars), a dedicated egg mode, and a cake bake setting. It also has a blue LCD screen that displays a cooking progress bar, giving you more at-a-glance feedback during longer cooks compared to the simpler LED readout on the Duo 7-in-1.
