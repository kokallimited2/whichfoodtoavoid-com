Do both scales come with batteries included?

Yes — both the ACCUWEIGHT 201 and the Amazon Basics ship with 2× AAA batteries in the box, so you can start weighing straight away without a trip to the shops.

Which scale is easier to read in a dark kitchen?

The ACCUWEIGHT 201 is noticeably better here — its backlit LCD remains clear in low-light conditions. The Amazon Basics uses a standard unlit LCD that can be tricky to read under dim or harsh overhead lighting.

Can these scales weigh liquids?
