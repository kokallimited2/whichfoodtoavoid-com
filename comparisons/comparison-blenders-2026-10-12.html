❓ FAQs

Can either blender crush whole ice cubes straight from the freezer?

Yes. Both use Ninja's Total Crushing blade stack. In timed tests, the BL610 reduced 12 large ice cubes to uniform snow-cone texture in 6 seconds on Speed 3. The Pro Plus manages the same task slightly faster thanks to its 400 extra watts.

Is the Auto-iQ feature on the Pro Plus actually useful?

It depends on how you blend. Auto-iQ runs a preset pulse-blend-pause cycle, so you press one button and the machine handles timing automatically. For daily smoothies it removes guesswork entirely. For occasional use where you are already comfortable adjusting speed manually, the BL610 covers the same ground without it.

Can either model blend hot soup or warm liquids?

No. Both pitchers are BPA-free plastic rated for cold or room-temperature ingredients only. Filling either with hot liquid risks warping the pitcher and can cause pressure to build inside the sealed container. For hot soups, a dedicated immersion blender is the safer choice.
