❓ FAQs

Which toaster is faster?

The Russell Hobbs 24381 is significantly faster thanks to its 1800W element — it typically toasts on a medium setting in under 2 minutes, whereas the Morphy Richards 222065 at 800W can take 3 to 4 minutes for the same result.

Can I toast just one slice in both models?

The Morphy Richards 222065 supports independent slot operation, so you can run a single slice without heating the second slot. The Russell Hobbs 24381 requires the left pair of slots to be active for the right pair to function, making true single-slice use less practical.

Do both toasters come with a warranty?

Yes — both carry a 2-year warranty. The Russell Hobbs covers you automatically, while the Morphy Richards requires you to register at morphyrichards.co.uk within 28 days of purchase to activate the full 2-year cover.
