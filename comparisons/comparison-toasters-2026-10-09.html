❓ FAQs

Can the Bella slim toaster fit bagels?

Not comfortably. The slot opening is too narrow for a standard bagel half lying flat. You can angle one in, but the result is uneven browning on one side and a higher risk of jamming the mechanism.

Does the Amazon Basics toaster have a reheat function?

No. It has a bagel mode, a defrost mode, and a cancel button, but no dedicated reheat setting. To warm already-toasted bread, set it to the lowest browning level and run a short cycle.

Which toaster is easier to clean?

Both have removable slide-out crumb trays. The Bella's single tray spans the full 10-inch slot with no central divider, making it marginally quicker to tip out and wipe down after use.
