Choose the Tassimo Suny if you want a no-fuss machine that covers lattes, hot chocolates, and teas without spending more than £30.

Philips L'OR Barista Sublime best for espresso lovers who want two cups at once and access to the full Nespresso pod ecosystem. Tassimo by Bosch Suny Special Edition best for budget shoppers and households who want drink variety beyond espresso — particularly milky coffees and hot chocolate.

❓ FAQs

Can the Philips L'OR Barista Sublime use Nespresso pods?

Yes — it's fully compatible with all Nespresso Original capsules, L'OR pods, and most third-party Nespresso-compatible capsules, giving you access to hundreds of coffee varieties from dozens of brands.

Does the Tassimo Suny make lattes and cappuccinos?

Yes — using T-Discs from Costa, Cadbury, and Kenco, it brews lattes, cappuccinos, teas, and hot chocolate; the milk component is included in a dedicated T-Disc, so no frothing or extra kit is required.

Which machine is cheaper to run day-to-day?

Tassimo T-Discs typically cost 25–50p per drink, while Nespresso-compatible pods used in the Philips start from around 20p — the Philips often works out cheaper per cup thanks to the wide market of competitively priced third-party Nespresso pods.
