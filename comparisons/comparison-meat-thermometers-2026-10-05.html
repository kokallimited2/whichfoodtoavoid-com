❓ FAQs

Is the Alpha Grillers thermometer accurate enough for food safety?

Yes, within ±1°F up to 392°F, which covers all standard cooking temperatures: chicken at 165°F, beef at 145°F, pork at 145°F. Accuracy drops above 392°F, but meat is already far beyond any safe-serving threshold at that point.

Does the ThermoWorks ThermoPop 2 work in Celsius?

Yes. A button press on both thermometers toggles between Fahrenheit and Celsius. The ThermoPop 2 also displays tenths of a degree, giving finer precision than the Alpha Grillers' whole-degree readout.

Which thermometer is better for oven use?

Both work for oven spot-checks, but neither is a leave-in probe. The ThermoPop 2's faster 2-3 second read reduces the time the oven door stays open, which is a practical advantage when checking a roast or a bake.
