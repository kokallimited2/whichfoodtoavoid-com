🏆 Verdict

If you bake bread at least twice a week or want a machine that grows with specialist attachments, the KitchenAid Artisan earns its £379.95. For lighter weekend baking, the Hamilton Beach at £151.95 handles cakes, meringues, and whipped cream without any meaningful compromise.

KitchenAid Artisan best for regular bakers who need heavy-duty dough handling, plan to add attachments, or can keep the machine out on the worktop permanently. Hamilton Beach best for occasional bakers, smaller households, or anyone who wants a reliable stand mixer under £160.
