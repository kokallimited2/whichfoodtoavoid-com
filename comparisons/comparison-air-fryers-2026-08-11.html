Is the AF400UK really bigger than the AF200UK?

Yes — the AF400UK holds 9.5L total across its two drawers (4.75L each), while the AF200UK holds 7.6L (roughly 3.8L each). That extra 1.9L makes a meaningful difference when cooking for four or more people.

Can both air fryers cook two different foods at the same time?

Yes. Both models feature Ninja's Sync and Match dual-zone system, letting you run each drawer independently with different temperatures, times, and cooking modes — finishing both at exactly the same moment.

Why does the newer AF200UK cost more than the AF400UK if it does less?

The AF200UK is a more recent release priced at launch rates. On pure value, the AF400UK wins clearly — unless you specifically need the slightly smaller footprint, there's no compelling reason to pay the premium for the AF200UK.
