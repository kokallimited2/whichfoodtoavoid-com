Does the Breville BTA820XL justify its £159.95 UK price?

For households making standard white or wholemeal toast, almost certainly not. The Breville justifies its price only if you bake thick loaves regularly and will use Lift and Look and A Bit More on a daily basis.

Which toaster handles bagels better?

The Breville BTA820XL is clearly the stronger choice for bagels. Its extra-wide slots fit full-size bagels without squeezing, and the bagel mode concentrates heat on the cut side while warming the outer crust. Why does the cheaper Cuisinart have a longer warranty than the Breville?

The Cuisinart CPT-122 carries a 3-year limited warranty while the Breville BTA820XL offers only 1 year. The Cuisinart's simpler construction has fewer mechanical parts to fail; the Breville's motorised carriage adds complexity that some owners report degrading after 12 to 24 months of daily use, making the warranty gap a meaningful factor at the Breville's price.
