❓ FAQs

Is the KitchenAid Artisan worth the extra £268 over the Aucma?

For weekly bakers, yes — the 5-year warranty, all-metal build, 10 speeds, and superior long-term reliability justify the premium. For occasional bakers (once a month or less), the Aucma delivers 80% of the performance for 27% of the cost.

Can the Aucma 6.2L handle bread dough?

Yes, but with caveats. Use the dough hook on speed 1 or 2, limit kneading sessions to 5–8 minutes, and let the motor rest between batches. Extended or high-hydration doughs may strain the motor over time. The KitchenAid handles bread dough more comfortably.

Which mixer is better for a small kitchen worktop?

The KitchenAid is heavier (approximately 11kg) but more compact and self-stable. The Aucma is lighter (4.7kg) and needs a smooth, grease-free surface for its suction feet to grip properly — if that fails, it can walk across the worktop. Both require similar footprint space.
