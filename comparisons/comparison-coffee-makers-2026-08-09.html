🏆 Verdict

Buy the De'Longhi Magnifica S if you're a genuine coffee drinker who wants proper fresh-ground espresso and lower cost per cup over time — it pays back its price against pods within a couple of years. Choose the TASSIMO Vivy 2 if you want the cheapest, most convenient way to make a wide variety of drinks without any fuss, or coffee is an occasional treat rather than a daily ritual.

The De'Longhi Magnifica S is best for daily espresso and cappuccino lovers who value freshly ground beans, control and long-term savings. The TASSIMO Vivy 2 is best for budget buyers, small kitchens and households wanting instant one-touch variety over café-grade quality.

❓ FAQs

Is the De'Longhi Magnifica S worth almost £280?

If you drink coffee daily, yes — freshly ground beans cost a fraction of pods, so the higher upfront price typically pays for itself within about two years while delivering noticeably better espresso and crema.

Does the TASSIMO Vivy 2 make real espresso?

Not in the traditional sense. It brews sealed T-Disc pods using barcode-controlled INTELLIBREW technology, giving fast, consistent drinks, but without the fresh-ground beans or thick crema of a bean-to-cup machine like the Magnifica S.

Which is cheaper to run day to day?

The Magnifica S. Whole beans work out far cheaper per cup than branded T-Discs, so despite its higher purchase price it's the lower long-term cost for regular coffee drinkers, whereas the Vivy 2's savings are all upfront.
