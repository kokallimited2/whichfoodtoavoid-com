❓ FAQs

Can either thermometer be used for jam, candy, or deep-frying oil?

Yes — both measure up to 300°C (572°F) and handle jam, sugar syrup, and frying oil perfectly. The key difference is that the ThermoPro TP03H is IPX6 waterproof so the whole unit can be rinsed after contact with sticky liquids, whereas the DOQAUS body must be wiped carefully to avoid moisture damage.

Which probe is better for a large Sunday roast joint?

The DOQAUS wins on probe length at 4.5 inches (vs the ThermoPro's 4 inches), and its ultra-slim 1.6 mm tip causes less puncture damage on presentation cuts. However, the ThermoPro's tighter ±0.5°C accuracy is the more important factor when you need to hit an exact internal temperature — such as 71°C for a whole chicken or 63°C for a medium pork loin.

How long do the batteries last on each model?

The ThermoPro TP03H runs on a single AAA battery, which typically lasts several months of regular home use, and the unit auto-shuts off after 10 minutes of inactivity. The DOQAUS uses a CR2032 coin cell with auto-on/off triggered by unfolding the probe; runtime is comparable but the CR2032 is harder to find locally when it needs replacing.
