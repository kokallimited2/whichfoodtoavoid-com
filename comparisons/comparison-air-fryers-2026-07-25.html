❓ FAQs

Can the Ninja AF400UK cook two different foods at the same time?

Yes. Its Dual Zone drawers let you use separate temperatures, times and functions, and the Sync feature ensures both finish together.

Is the Tower Vortx 4L big enough for a family?

It comfortably serves one to two people; for a family of four or more, the 9.5L Ninja is a much better fit.

Which air fryer is cheaper to run?

The Tower draws less power (1400W vs 2470W) so uses less energy per session, but the Ninja can cook two things at once, saving time and often energy overall for bigger meals.
