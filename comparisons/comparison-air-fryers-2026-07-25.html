❓ FAQs

What's the real difference between the AF300UK and AF400UK?

The core features are identical — both have two independent zones, 6 cooking functions, and SYNC/MATCH. The AF400UK simply has larger drawers (2 x 4.75L vs 2 x 3.8L) for a 9.5L total versus 7.6L, plus a marginally higher 2470W power and a bigger footprint.

Is the extra £30 for the 9.5L model worth it?

Only if you regularly cook for six or more people or want to fit larger items like a bigger chicken or extra roast potatoes. For households of up to four or five, the cheaper 7.6L AF300UK offers the same performance and better value.

Do both air fryers come with a warranty?

Yes. Both come with a 2-year guarantee, but you must register the product with Ninja (typically within 28 days of purchase) to activate the full cover.
