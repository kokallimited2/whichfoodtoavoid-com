Can the MEATER SE go fully in the oven or on the grill?

Yes — the probe is 100% wireless and designed to stay inside the meat in the oven, grill or smoker, with the charger acting as a Bluetooth repeater to your phone up to 50m away.

Is the ThermoPro TP19H accurate enough for cooking meat safely?

Yes. It reads in about 1 second with roughly ±0.5°C accuracy across a -50°C to 300°C range, which is more than enough to hit safe internal temperatures for poultry, pork and beef.

Is the MEATER SE worth nearly 7x the price of the TP19H?

Only if you value hands-free, app-guided cooking and remote alerts. For pure accuracy and quick checks the TP19H does the core job for under £12; the MEATER SE adds convenience, not better safety.
