❓ FAQs

Can the Ninja AF100UK cook a whole meal for a family?

It handles 2-4 portions, so it suits couples or small families, but larger households cooking a main plus sides will find the 9.5L AF400UK far more practical.

What does the Dual Zone feature on the AF400UK actually do?

It gives you two separate drawers with independent temperature and timer settings, and the Sync function ensures both finish cooking at the same moment so your whole meal is ready together.

Do both air fryers need oil?

No. Both use rapid hot-air circulation to crisp food with little or no oil, making them a healthier alternative to deep frying.
