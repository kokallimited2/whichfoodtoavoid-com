❓ FAQs

Can the Ninja Air Fryer cook a whole chicken?

No. The 3.8L basket handles chicken thighs, wings, or a small 1.2 kg joint, but not a whole bird. The Emeril fits a 6lb (2.7 kg) whole chicken comfortably.

Does the Emeril French Door Air Fryer need preheating?

Yes. Most functions benefit from a 3 to 5 minute preheat. That is faster than a conventional oven by around 10 minutes, but slower than the Ninja, which skips preheating entirely.

Which costs more to run?

The Emeril draws 1700W versus the Ninja's 1550W. Running both for 30 minutes daily, the Emeril uses roughly 27 extra kWh per year, adding about £7.50 to your electricity bill at October 2026 UK unit rates.
