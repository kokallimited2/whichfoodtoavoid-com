❓ FAQs

What is the difference between Sync and Match on these Ninja air fryers?

Sync coordinates two drawers cooking at different temperatures and times so both finish simultaneously — ideal for cooking a main dish and a side together. Match simply copies your first drawer's settings to the second, which is handy when cooking a larger quantity of the same food across both zones.

Is the Ninja AF400UK too large for a standard UK kitchen?

At 415mm wide and 8.8kg it is bulky — measure your worktop before ordering. The AF300UK is around 4cm narrower and roughly 2.6kg lighter, which makes a genuine difference if storage space is at a premium.

Can either of these air fryers replace a full-sized oven?

For most everyday cooking — roasting chicken, crisping chips, reheating leftovers — yes, and both do it up to 75% faster than a conventional fan oven while using less energy. For very large joints, a whole turkey, or tall bakes that need oven height, you will still need your main oven.
