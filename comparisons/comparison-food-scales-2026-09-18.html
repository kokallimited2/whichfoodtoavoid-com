❓ FAQs

Are these scales accurate enough for bread baking or weighing yeast?

Both measure in 1-gram increments and stay within ±1 g for weights above roughly 5 g — perfectly fine for flour, butter, sugar, and most bread ingredients. Neither is reliable for amounts below 2–3 g, so for tiny yeast or spice doses, a dedicated pocket or jewellery scale would give you a more trustworthy result.

Which scale is easier to clean after a floury baking session?

The Etekcity has a clear advantage here: its stainless-steel platform lifts off entirely so you can rinse it separately and get into the edges. The Amazon Basics platform is fixed to the body, meaning spilled liquids or fine powders can work their way into the housing and are harder to clear out.

Can I use either scale to weigh liquids for UK recipes that call for millilitres?

Yes — both include an mL mode that converts weight to volume assuming water density (1 mL = 1 g), which is accurate for water, milk, and most thin liquids. For denser ingredients like honey, golden syrup, or oil, measuring in grams gives a more precise result than relying on the mL conversion.
