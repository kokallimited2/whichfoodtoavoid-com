🏆 Verdict

For most UK buyers, the COSORI TurboBlaze is the sharper purchase: at the same price it delivers a PFAS-free basket, a two-year warranty, nine cooking modes, and a quieter DC motor. Buy the Ninja Air Fryer Pro XL if you regularly cook for four or more people and the extra 0.5 litres of batch capacity matters more to you than coating chemistry, or if you want the simplest possible machine with minimal learning time.

COSORI TurboBlaze 9-in-1 best for households prioritising PFAS-free cooking, quieter operation, a longer warranty, and versatile modes including bread proving and dehydrating. Ninja Air Fryer Pro XL 6-in-1 best for larger households wanting the biggest basket at this price and a no-fuss six-button interface for fast weeknight cooking.

Does the COSORI TurboBlaze have a PFAS-free basket?

Yes. The TurboBlaze basket and crisper plate use a PFAS-free ceramic nonstick coating with no PTFE or PFOA. Both parts are dishwasher safe.

Which air fryer has the longer warranty in the UK?

The COSORI TurboBlaze comes with a 2-year manufacturer warranty. The Ninja Air Fryer Pro XL AF181 carries a 1-year limited warranty, which is standard for Ninja basket air fryers.

How much quieter is the COSORI TurboBlaze than the Ninja?

COSORI rates the TurboBlaze at under 53 dB at its highest fan speed, because of the DC brushless motor. Ninja does not publish a decibel figure for the AF181, but conventional AC-motor air fryers in this size class typically measure 60-65 dB during operation.
