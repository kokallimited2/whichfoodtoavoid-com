🏆 Verdict

Buy the AF400UK if you cook for a family and want to prepare mains and sides together without juggling batches — the Dual Zone convenience justifies the extra cost. If you live alone or as a couple and want to save money and counter space, the AF100UK delivers the same great Ninja crisping in a smaller, cheaper package.

The AF100UK is best for singles, couples and small kitchens on a budget. The AF400UK is best for families and batch cookers who want to cook two dishes at once.

❓ FAQs

What is the difference between the AF100UK and AF400UK?

The AF100UK is a single 3.8L basket with 4 functions, while the AF400UK has two separate 4.75L drawers (9.5L total) and 6 functions including Dual Zone Sync and Match cooking.

Can the Ninja AF400UK cook two different foods at once?

Yes. Its Dual Zone design lets each drawer run a different food, time and temperature, and the Sync setting finishes both at the same moment.

Is the AF100UK big enough for a family?

It comfortably serves 1-2 people. For 3 or more, or for cooking mains and sides together, the larger 9.5L AF400UK is the better choice.
