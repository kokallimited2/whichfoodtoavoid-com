Is the Cosori TurboBlaze sold in the UK with a UK plug?

Yes. The UK model (CAF-DC601-KUK) is rated 220-240V and ships with a UK plug. Cosori sells it direct via cosori.co.uk and through major retailers. It also carries a two-year warranty, a year longer than the Ninja Pro's standard cover.

Which is better for a small UK kitchen worktop?

The Ninja Air Fryer Pro measures 28.5 x 36 x 26.5cm and fits under most standard UK wall units. The Cosori TurboBlaze footprint is roughly 30 x 36.6 x 30.2cm, so if cupboard clearance is under 30cm, the Ninja is the safer fit.

Does the Cosori TurboBlaze basket actually avoid PFAS chemicals?

Cosori certifies the TurboBlaze ceramic coating as PFAS-free, meaning none of the per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances associated with traditional PTFE non-stick coatings. The Ninja Air Fryer Pro uses a standard non-stick coating and does not carry equivalent certification.
