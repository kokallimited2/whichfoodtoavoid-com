What to Look for Before You Buy

Motor wattage determines how well a blender handles ice and frozen fruit. A 900W motor covers most smoothie tasks; 1,200W gives headroom for fibrous greens blended every day. Cup size matters too: 20 to 24 oz suits a single serving, while 32 oz works for batch prep.

One Habit That Extends Any Blender's Life

Add liquid before solids. Pouring 4 oz of water or milk in first pulls frozen chunks toward the blade instead of jamming them against it. This single step reduces motor strain on any budget model rated below 1,000W.
