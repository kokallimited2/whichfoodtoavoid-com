Frequently asked questions

What does tare mean on a digital kitchen scale?

Tare zeroes the display while a container sits on the platform. You add each ingredient in turn without lifting the bowl, and the scale subtracts the container weight automatically.

How accurate are cheap digital kitchen scales in grams?

Most scales priced between $5 and $15 read to 1g accuracy, which is sufficient for baking and cooking. For coffee or spice work, look for models labeled 0.1g or 3kg/0.1g.

Can I use a kitchen scale for smoothie and protein shake recipes?

Yes. Weighing protein powder, frozen berries, and nut butter in grams is more consistent than measuring by cup. A 1g-accuracy scale is all you need for blender recipes.
