Frequently asked questions

Are glass food storage containers with lids safe for the oven?

Borosilicate glass containers are oven-safe; always remove the plastic or silicone lid before placing any container in the oven. Check the individual product listing for the exact temperature rating before use.

Do airtight lids actually prevent leaks in a bag?

Snap-lock lids with a silicone gasket hold a proper seal when a container is laid flat or on its side. Press-fit lids without a locking mechanism work loose under bag pressure.

How many containers do I need for weekly meal prep?

For five lunches and five dinners, a 10-pack at 22 to 30 oz per container covers a full week from one prep session. Larger households typically need two packs.

Can glass food storage containers go in the dishwasher?

All glass sets on this list are dishwasher-safe; lids should go on the top rack only. Repeated bottom-rack heat can warp plastic lid components over time.
