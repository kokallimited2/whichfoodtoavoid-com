What to Look for in a Sourdough Scale

Sourdough baking requires two things from a scale: enough capacity for a full batch of dough (most recipes reach 1,500 to 2,000 grams) and a readability of 1g or finer. A pull-out or raised display helps when a large mixing bowl blocks the screen. Tare is non-negotiable because you weigh each ingredient into the same bowl without lifting it.

One Practical Tip Before You Start Weighing

Place the scale on a firm, level surface before you zero it. Even a slight lean can throw readings off by 2 to 5 grams on budget models. For starter additions under 10 grams, the dual-platform models (KUAIVO, KKZ, Digital Dual Platform) give better precision than a single wide platform.

Do you need a scale to make sourdough bread?

Yes. Sourdough hydration is calculated by weight, not volume, so cup measurements introduce too much variation for consistent results. A basic 1g-increment scale like the Ultrean ($6.98) is the minimum you need.

What weight capacity do I need in a sourdough scale?
