What to Look For in a Kitchen Scale

Capacity and increment size are the two numbers that matter most. A 0.1g increment suits baking and nutrition tracking; 0.001oz precision is for coffee, spice blends, or medication dosing. Display position also counts: a top-mounted screen gets blocked by large bowls, which is why pull-out or angled displays are worth paying for.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most accurate kitchen scale for food?

The KUAIVO and KKZ both read to 0.001oz, which covers coffee brewing and fine nutrition tracking. For standard baking, a 0.1g increment like the GRAM PRES is accurate enough for most recipes.

Can I use a kitchen food scale to measure liquids?

Yes. Place a container on the scale, press tare to zero it, then pour. Water weighs 1g per milliliter, so this method works reliably for most liquid ingredients without needing a measuring jug.

What capacity do I need in a kitchen scale for food?

Most home cooks are fine with 11lb to 22lb. If you batch-cook or weigh full stockpots, a 33lb model like the KUAIVO or KKZ gives more headroom before the scale maxes out.
