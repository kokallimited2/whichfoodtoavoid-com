What to Look for in a Baking Scale

Capacity and resolution are the two numbers that matter. A 22 lb limit covers large bread doughs and double batches of cookie mix, while 1 g resolution handles standard recipes without issue. If you weigh espresso shots or small spice additions, step up to a model rated at 0.1 g precision.

One Habit That Gets More from Any Scale

Use the tare function between every ingredient, not just at the start. Put your bowl on the scale, press tare, add the first ingredient, press tare again, then add the next. Skipping that step is where cumulative errors creep in.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best digital kitchen scale for baking?

For most home bakers, any scale with at least 22 lb capacity, a tare function, and 1 g resolution covers every standard recipe. The OXO Good Grips and Nicewell both score 4.7 stars and meet those specifications.

Do I need grams or ounces for baking?

Most professional recipes, and nearly all UK recipes, use grams. Every scale on this list reads both units, so you switch with one button press depending on where the recipe comes from.

How accurate does a baking scale need to be?

One gram is fine for bread and cake. If you weigh espresso shots or spice blends in small amounts, look for 0.1 g resolution instead.
