Frequently asked questions

Are glass food storage containers better than plastic for meal prep?

Glass does not absorb odors or stain after repeated reheating, which plastic typically does within six months of regular use. Borosilicate glass also moves straight from freezer to oven, which most plastic containers cannot handle safely.

Can glass food storage containers go in the dishwasher?

Most borosilicate glass containers are dishwasher-safe on the top rack. The plastic snap-lock lids can warp on the bottom rack over time, so top-rack washing extends lid life considerably.

How many food storage containers do I need for weekly meal prep?

Ten containers cover five lunches and five dinners for one person. Cooking for two people, or adding snack portions, means a 20-piece set is closer to what you will actually use.

Are glass food storage containers safe for the freezer and oven?

Borosilicate glass is designed for both freezer storage and oven use up to roughly 400 to 450°F depending on the specific product. Always remove plastic lids before putting the containers in the oven.
