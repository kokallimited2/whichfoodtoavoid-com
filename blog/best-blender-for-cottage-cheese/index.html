What to Look for in a Blender for Cottage Cheese

Aim for at least 700W. Cold cottage cheese is dense, and a weaker motor will stall or leave visible lumps. Blade design matters too: extraction-style blades pull thick ingredients into the cutting zone, while wide chopper blades can spin around them.

A Small Tip That Cuts Blend Time in Half

Add two tablespoons of water or milk before blending cottage cheese. That small amount of liquid gives the blades room to move against the dense curd structure. Blend time drops noticeably, and the result is smoother.
