Frequently asked questions

What should I look for in a kitchen scale for baking?

At minimum, you need 1-gram accuracy and a tare function to zero out bowl weight. A backlit display is a practical addition if your counter sits in a darker corner of the kitchen.

Is a budget kitchen scale accurate enough for baking?

For bread, cakes, and cookies, yes. Budget scales typically measure to 1 gram, which covers most standard recipes. Where they fall short is quantities under 2 grams, such as small measures of yeast, where 0.1g precision is more reliable.

What is the difference between a single platform and a dual platform kitchen scale?

A single platform handles one bowl at a time, which covers most baking tasks. A dual platform model adds a small secondary tray for fine ingredients like salt or baking powder, so you do not need to switch vessels mid-recipe.

Why does my kitchen scale give different readings each time?

Low batteries cause most reading drift in digital scales, so replace them first. The second most common cause is an uneven surface: move the scale to a flat counter and re-zero before concluding the unit is faulty.
