What to Look for in a Smoothie and Ice Blender

Motor wattage matters most when crushing ice, look for at least 600 watts to avoid motor strain on frozen ingredients. Cup size is equally important: personal blenders in the 14–32 oz range suit individuals, while combo units with a larger pitcher work better for households. One Tip That Makes Any Blender Work Better on Ice

Always add liquid before frozen ingredients, even two tablespoons of water or milk at the base prevents the blade from grinding against solid ice with no movement. This single habit protects the motor and produces a smoother result regardless of which model you own.
