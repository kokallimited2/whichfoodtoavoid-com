One Tip That Makes Any Blender Work Better

Add liquid before solid ingredients. Pouring water, milk, or juice in first lets the blade catch it immediately, which stops air pockets from forming and keeps the motor from laboring. For frozen smoothies, let ingredients sit at room temperature for five minutes before blending at full speed.
